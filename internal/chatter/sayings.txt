# What the pet says to itself when nobody has sent it anything.
#
# One saying per line. Blank lines are ignored, and a line starting with # is a
# comment, like this one. Point behavior.chatter.source at your own file to
# replace the lot — this one is only here so the feature does something on a
# fresh install.
#
# Mostly philosophers, who are long out of copyright and reliably quotable, and
# a few musicians who said something worth repeating. Quotes are given in the
# forms they are usually circulated in; the rock ones especially are hard to
# source to a particular interview, so treat the wording as approximate.
#
# No song lyrics. A line from a song is somebody's work, not a saying.
